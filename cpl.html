About this site

The questions and answers on this site are taken from aviation textbooks and study guides. Most of those books are older editions.

Do not rely on this site for topics that change. Air law, regulations, aircraft performance figures, limitations and procedures change over time, so an older book may give an answer that is no longer correct. Use the date of the source as a guide.

Where a source has been uploaded, its date is noted when known. If you are not sure how old a source is, treat its content as unverified.

For current rules and exam content, check the current CASA regulations, the current syllabus and your flight training organisation's materials.

This site is for study practice only. It is not an official CASA exam resource.
